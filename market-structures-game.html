The colours in every chart

Consumer surplus

What buyers were willing to pay minus what they actually paid. The area under demand and above the price.

Producer surplus

Revenue minus variable cost. The area above marginal cost and below the price. Economic profit is producer surplus minus fixed cost.

Deadweight loss

Surplus nobody gets because output is below (or above) the efficient level where price equals marginal cost.

Total surplus = consumer surplus + producer surplus. Its largest possible value is reached where demand meets marginal cost; deadweight loss is the gap between that maximum and what the market achieves. The game uses linear demand and simple cost curves, so all numbers are illustrative.

The big picture

As market power rises, firms push price further above marginal cost. The Lerner index, (P − MC) ÷ P, moves from zero under perfect competition toward its highest values under monopoly or a successful cartel.

Barriers to entry decide whether profit survives. Free entry wiped out profit in perfect and monopolistic competition; licences, economies of scale and patents protected it in oligopoly and monopoly.

Market power moves surplus from buyers to sellers and destroys some of it altogether. Deadweight loss is zero only when output reaches the level where price equals marginal cost.
